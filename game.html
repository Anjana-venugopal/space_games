🇮🇳 Chandrayaan-3 & Artemis Synergy

In August 2023, ISRO's Chandrayaan-3 Vikram lander became the first spacecraft to soft-land near the lunar south pole (69.37° S), measuring topsoil temperatures with ChaSTE and confirming sulfur with LIBS.

🧊 Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs)

Because the Moon's axis is tilted only 1.54°, deep polar crater floors never see sunlight and stay below 100 K (-173°C), trapping ancient water ice that can be split into H₂ fuel and O₂ breathing air.

☀️ Peaks of Eternal Light

Adjacent high crater rims at Shackleton receive solar illumination for 85%–90% of the lunar year, providing nearly continuous solar power right next to shadowed ice reservoirs.
